# CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_QUESTIONS

Use these questions to test product hypotheses. No customer interviews, commitments, or deployments are represented by this repository.

## Process and quality

1. Which continuous line and product family would be in scope for an initial investigation?
2. Which quality failures cause the most rework, scrap, or downtime, and how are they currently detected?
3. What are the line's normal process envelopes, changeovers, and known sources of drift?
4. Which signals are available, at what cadence, with what calibration and data-retention constraints?
5. How are defect events labeled and linked to process history? Who reviews ambiguous labels?

## Workflow and safety

6. Which roles review alerts, and what evidence is needed before an operator acts?
7. What are acceptable false-alarm and missed-event costs for a read-only warning?
8. Which existing alarm, historian, MES, or quality systems would need an approved integration?
9. What safety, cybersecurity, privacy, and change-management reviews are mandatory?
10. What actions must remain strictly human-approved and outside automatic control?

## Pilot and economics

11. What baseline period and prospective evaluation would be credible?
12. Which metrics and acceptance thresholds should be agreed before a pilot?
13. What costs belong in a customer ROI calculation (scrap, energy, labor, downtime, integration, and ongoing operations)?
14. Who owns the data, validation decision, deployment approval, and ongoing model monitoring?
