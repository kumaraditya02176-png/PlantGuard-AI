# PRODUCT_OVERVIEW

PlantGuard AI is a simulation-only proof-of-concept for physics-aware quality-risk monitoring in continuous manufacturing, demonstrated with plastic extrusion.

The demo generates nine process signals under six scenarios, predicts synthetic risk for surface cracking, warping, and thickness variation, and displays the leading risk with an advisory. Users can apply a bounded correction to the simulator, compare PINN and LSTM outputs, enter their own illustrative ROI assumptions, and export recent sensor history as CSV.

The app does not read a real plant, use empirically validated defect labels, guarantee savings, or control equipment. Current scores are simulator evaluations and must not be interpreted as production performance. A real deployment would require plant data, validation, calibration, security and safety reviews, and supervised trials.
