# COMMERCIAL_USE_CASES

## Potential discovery areas

These are hypotheses for customer discovery, not validated customer needs or available product capabilities:

- Early warning for extrusion quality drift associated with temperature, pressure, cooling, feed, vibration, viscosity, or thickness.
- Consolidating scenario trends and defect-specific risk into a single operator-facing view.
- Comparing physics-informed and temporal model predictions during a supervised, read-only trial.
- Estimating the economics of scrap-reduction assumptions with an explicitly illustrative calculator.

## Current demonstrator boundary

Only the simulated plastic-extrusion workflow is implemented. It uses synthetic measurements and labels, and its correction feedback changes simulator state only. No evidence here establishes performance for extrusion plants, other continuous processes, or customer operating conditions.

## Evidence required before a use case is claimed

Confirm process ownership and user workflow; secure data access and labeling; establish baseline false-alarm and missed-event costs; agree on a prospective evaluation protocol; validate sensor coverage and model calibration; and obtain safety, security, legal, and operational approval. Do not describe projected savings as achieved outcomes.
